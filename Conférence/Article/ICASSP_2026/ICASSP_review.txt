Concernant les pondérations spatiale et temporelle.
Les pondérations proposées reposent sur des principes esthétiques et narratifs issus de la pratique et de la théorie cinématographiques (composition centrale, structuration dramatique des plans). L’objectif n’est pas de modéliser statistiquement toutes les formes de cinéma, mais de proposer un cadre simple, explicite et cohérent avec des conventions largement observées, en particulier dans le cinéma d’auteur.

We would like to thank the reviewers for their careful reading and constructive comments, which have enabled us to clarify the positioning and methodological choices of our work. We respond below to the main questions raised.

Regarding the dataset and evaluation protocol.
The test set is deliberately composed exclusively of shots from films, in accordance with the objective explicitly stated in the title and the positioning of the article, which targets cinema and not generic video. The number of shots evaluated is indeed limited; this choice is the result of a conscious tradeoff between the diversity of the works and the nature of the evaluation. In the context of cinema, there is no single truth for selecting an image that is representative of a shot. An evaluation based on a significant number of film analysis experts seemed more relevant to us than a comparison with a single, potentially arbitrary pseudo-reference for an inherently subjective task.

Regarding spatial and temporal weightings.
The proposed weightings are based on aesthetic and narrative principles derived from cinematographic practice and theory (central composition, dramatic structuring of shots). They are deliberately interpretable and heuristic, rather than learned, which corresponds to the positioning of the work. The aim is not to statistically model all forms of cinema, but to propose a simple, explicit framework that is consistent with widely observed conventions, particularly in auteur cinema.

Regarding the object detector.
We agree with the observation that newer detectors could improve performance or execution speed. However, the detector used is not a central element of the contribution: the method is independent of the detection model and can be based on any detector providing scores by class, with the K parameter adapting accordingly. This choice is primarily for clarity and reproducibility.

Regarding ablation, complexity, and parameter selection.
Internal experiments were conducted to evaluate the relevance of different weightings and the choice of their parameters. Given the short format of this article, we have chosen not to detail these analyses. Furthermore, in the context of cinema, shots rarely exceed one minute; simple temporal sampling strategies can therefore significantly reduce the cost of computation if necessary.

Finally, if the paper is accepted, we will correct the linguistic errors and the citation error identified, and adapt the format to IEEE standards to ensure a clear presentation that meets the conference's expectations.
